EU climate law, made legible

EU climate legislation is a dense network of directives, regulations, delegated acts and amendments layered over two decades, making it hard to see through — not only for newcomers, but for anyone working outside their own corner of it.

This site visualises core features of that system: what legislation actually says, how individual acts connect to one another, and how it has changed over time. It is meant as an orientation tool and a starting point for your own research, not a substitute for the legal texts. Every figure and every act links back to its official source.

How to use this

Orientation, not legal advice

Everything here is compiled from the Official Journal and Commission sources, and each figure and act links back to its original text on EUR-Lex. The visualisations necessarily simplify: they group, round and summarise. Where a judgement call was needed — how to classify an amending act, which acts fall inside the scope, how to read an ambiguous provision — the reasoning is set out on the page itself rather than hidden.

Before relying on anything here for compliance, filings or contracts, check the consolidated text on EUR-Lex. Legislation moves quickly, and several of the acts shown are under active revision.

Built as an independent, non-commercial resource. Sources: EUR-Lex, the European Commission's climate pages, and the Publications Office of the EU. Last reviewed August 2026.
