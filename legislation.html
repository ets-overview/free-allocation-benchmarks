About this map

Mapping climate legislation presents a particular challenge because of the broad and cross-cutting nature of climate policy. Many legal acts are only partially related to climate change, even though they may have significant implications for climate mitigation. Energy policy, for example, overlaps extensively with climate policy.

To keep the overview clear, I have consciously tried to include only legislation that addresses climate change as a primary objective. Given the already large number of legal acts covered, including all indirectly relevant legislation would make the map considerably more complex and difficult to navigate.

Furthermore, the starting point of this map has been determined somewhat arbitrarily. For carbon pricing, the timeline begins with the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS), while in other policy areas selected pieces of legislation serve as starting points. The main objective at this stage is to present the current landscape of climate legislation. Older legislation may be added in the future to better illustrate the historical development of climate policy over time.

If you believe that important legislation is missing, please feel free to get in touch at eu-climate-overview@pm.me.
